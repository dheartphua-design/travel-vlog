Behind the camera

Slow travel, one carry-on, no itinerary.

Far Afield is a travel vlog about the places between the famous ones. I film the overnight trains, the market stalls before they open, and the conversations that happen when a plan falls apart.

New episodes go up every other Sunday. Each one is shot on a single trip, edited on the road, and comes with a free guide to where I stayed, ate, and got lost.
