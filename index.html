    Dear viewer,

    I film the overnight trains, the market stalls before they open, and the conversations that happen when a plan falls apart. No itinerary, one carry-on, and usually a little lost.

    Each episode is shot on a single trip and edited on the road. The description always links to where I stayed, what I ate, and what I'd skip next time.
